ABOUT THE VIDYALAYA

A place to discover potential.

Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, Puto, Koderma is a Central Government residential school under Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti.

The Vidyalaya is affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) and provides education from Class VI to Class XII.

The school combines academics with sports, cultural activities, practical learning, library resources and residential campus life.

“Education is not only about marks — it is about becoming capable.”
